If there are questions about the reliability of
the eqdsk file, for example after checking the graphics
in files dR.ps dZ.ps for plots of the magnetic
field components, then the geoq command prints in its log a
variety of outputs for psi on limiter, psi of X-point etc.
which might help.
See the web-based documentation for beqparameters as to how to
produce these outputs.
